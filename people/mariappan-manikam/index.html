Professional Summary

Mariappan is an Associate at Rajpal, Firah & Vishnu with 13 years of experience in criminal litigation. He is also a YBGK lawyer, providing free legal services to financially struggling Malaysians. His YBGK work includes bail and mitigation at Magistrates’ Courts, and criminal trials assigned by YBGK.

His approach is analytical and detail-oriented. Having served in the Royal Malaysia Police from 1994 to 2005, reaching the rank of Assistant Superintendent of Police, he brings a distinct perspective on how criminal matters are investigated and prosecuted.

Clients can expect clear, approachable guidance throughout their matter.
