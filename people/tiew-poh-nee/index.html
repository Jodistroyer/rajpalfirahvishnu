Professional Summary

Tiew Poh Nee is an Associate at Rajpal, Firah & Vishnu with 10+ years of experience in criminal litigation. She tailors a legal strategy to the specific facts, objectives, and circumstances of each client. Clients are kept informed throughout the process, legal issues are explained in plain language, and options are set out clearly at every stage.

She is best known for sharp legal research and excellent drafting, with a particular focus on written submissions. Clients can expect clear advice, regular updates, fast responses, and supportive guidance throughout their matter.

Tiew Poh Nee has assisted lead counsel in multiple trials involving drug cases, murder, AMLA cases, SOSMA, ATIPSOM, and income tax matters. Her work includes trial preparation and drafting written submissions across these matters.
