Professional Summary

Every case starts with understanding the client’s goals, concerns, and the facts. Siti Anis takes the time to learn what matters most before recommending a strategy, and provides an honest assessment of each case, including both opportunities and potential challenges, so clients can make informed decisions.

She is best known for sharp legal research and excellent drafting, with a particular focus on written submissions. Clients can expect clear advice, regular updates, fast responses, and supportive guidance throughout their matter.
